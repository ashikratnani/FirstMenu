# FirstMenu demo script (under 3 minutes)

A timed walkthrough for the hackathon video. Target length: about 2:45 of spoken
content. Scene cues are in **bold**; the lines after each cue are what to say
while showing it. The maintainer records and uploads the video; this is just the
script.

Before recording: open the live app at
[https://ashikratnani.github.io/FirstMenu/](https://ashikratnani.github.io/FirstMenu/)
on a phone-width window, and have a fresh profile ready (clear `localStorage`) so
onboarding shows.

---

**[0:00 – 0:20] Open on the home screen and the one-liner**

> "This is FirstMenu, a free web app for planning a week of baby meals for
> babies about 6 to 12 months old. No account, no backend. Everything stays in
> your browser. And it's not medical advice, so always check with your
> pediatrician."

**[0:20 – 0:45] Setup / onboarding**

- Show the first-run disclaimer, then enter a birth date that makes the baby
  about 10 months old and an optional name.
- Check off a couple of already-tried foods.

> "Setup takes a few seconds: a birth date, an optional name, and the foods
> you've already tried. From the age, FirstMenu knows the stage and which foods
> are safe yet."

**[0:45 – 1:05] A ghosted honey recipe**

- Open the recipe library and show a recipe that contains honey appearing as a
  greyed-out *ghost* that can't be planned, with the reason visible.

> "Here's the safety screen. A recipe with honey shows up as a ghost. It can't
> be planned, and it tells you why: honey can cause infant botulism under one
> year. Every rule like this cites a published source you can read on the
> Sources page."

**[1:05 – 1:25] The grapes reminder**

- Open a recipe that uses grapes and show the prep reminder.

> "Some foods are fine but need prep. Grapes carry a reminder to quarter them
> lengthwise before serving, right on the recipe, so nothing gets missed."

**[1:25 – 1:45] A first-taste door opening**

- Go to the Foods page. Point out a *mystery door* for an untried allergen, then
  tap "tried, no reaction" and show the door open into a friendly monster.

> "First tastes are tracked as mystery doors. When you introduce a food and mark
> it tried with no reaction, the door opens into a friendly monster. If you ever
> note a reaction, recipes with that food turn back into ghosts until you clear
> it."

**[1:45 – 2:10] Build the week plan**

- Open the week view and add a couple of eligible recipes into breakfast and
  dinner slots. Show that ghosted recipes can't be dropped in.

> "Planning the week is a plain, bright grid. Eligible recipes come first, and
> anything unsafe for the baby's age just can't be added. In a few taps you've
> got the week filled in."

**[2:10 – 2:35] Prep list and grocery list**

- Open the Prep page (cook sessions, storage times), then the Grocery page
  (merged, grouped, with check-off).

> "From that plan, FirstMenu builds a make-ahead prep list with batch sizes and
> fridge and freezer times, and a grocery list merged by item and grouped by
> aisle, with check-off as you shop."

**[2:35 – 2:50] Share link**

- Open the Share page, copy the read-only link, and open it (ideally on a second
  device) to show the read-only view.

> "And you can share the week as a read-only link. The plan is packed into the
> link itself, with no name or birth date, so whoever's cooking can follow along
> or print it out. That's FirstMenu."

---

Total: ~2:50. If it runs long, trim the grapes beat (1:05–1:25) or the setup
narration first.
