# Design inspiration

Popular baby and family food sites, reviewed on November 12, 2026 at 390 px (phone) and 1280 px (desktop). Use them for patterns only. Don't copy their look, text, images or logos.

Screenshots were taken during the research session and are not committed, because they show other companies' copyrighted pages and this repo is public under MIT. To see a page, open its link at 390 px and 1280 px with the browser tool.

## Closest to FirstMenu

### Solid Starts: First Foods Database
- https://solidstarts.com/foods/ and a food page, for example https://solidstarts.com/foods/egg/
- **Search first.** The database page leads with one large search box on a soft tinted band.
- **Food facts as a short table.** A food page opens with three labelled rows: age suggestion, iron-rich, common allergen, each with a small help icon. This maps directly to FirstMenu's stage, Iron-rich tag and allergen door.
- **Calm palette.** Navy text on a light periwinkle band, white cards, big rounded photo. Generous spacing.
- **Watch out:** a very long disclaimer block above the content. FirstMenu's disclaimer should stay short and in the footer.

### NHS Best Start in Life: recipes and meal ideas
- https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/recipes-and-meal-ideas/ and a recipe, for example https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/recipes-and-meal-ideas/chicken-noodle-stew/
- **Recipe facts up front:** "Age: 10 to 12 months · Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 25 mins · Portions: 4" right under the title, before ingredients. FirstMenu's time, effort, stage and tags belong there too.
- **Plain, readable type**, clear breadcrumb, strong focus states, accessible by default. A good model for the recipe page.
- **Ingredients and method side by side** on desktop, stacked on phone.

### MyFridgeFood: "What's in your fridge?"
- https://www.myfridgefood.com/
- The same core idea as the Make screen: tick ingredients, see a "Your ingredients" summary, press "Find recipes".
- **Learn from what doesn't work:** long checkbox grids, ads and app banners push the tool below the fold, and the button sits apart from the list. FirstMenu should put the search box and results on the first screen, update results live, and show chips instead of a checkbox wall.

### Mealime (shutting down October 2026)
- https://www.mealime.com/
- Its app screenshots show the pattern to borrow: horizontal rows of photo cards ("Most popular", "Recently created") with "See all". FirstMenu's "Make again" row can use the same row-of-cards shape with SVG art instead of photos.

## Brand and tone

### Feeding Littles
- https://feedinglittles.com/
- Warm sage, coral and cream; rounded sans-serif; one coral primary button per screen. Close to FirstMenu's palette and a good reference for a friendly, confident tone.

### Tiny Organics
- https://www.tinyorganics.com/
- Soft pastel bands, deep navy text, one big pill-shaped primary button, short reassuring lines ("no prep, no pressure"). Good for button shape and microcopy rhythm.

### Kids Eat in Color
- https://kidseatincolor.com/
- Bright, playful wordmark and one strong orange button. Reference for using colour to signal food groups (FirstMenu's nutrition tags).

### Cerebelly and Little Spoon
- https://cerebelly.com/ and https://www.littlespoon.com/
- Shopping sites: pastel backgrounds, rounded white cards with an arrow, a sticky full-width primary button at the bottom on phones (Little Spoon). The sticky bottom button suits FirstMenu's Try this and Save actions.
- **Avoid:** sale banners, chat bubbles, carousels and product photography. FirstMenu sells nothing.

## Patterns to use in FirstMenu

1. **One search box, first thing on the Make screen** (Solid Starts, MyFridgeFood done better).
2. **Fact rows right under a title:** stage, time, effort, iron-rich, new foods (Solid Starts, NHS).
3. **Rounded white cards on a soft tinted band**, generous spacing, one accent colour (Solid Starts, Feeding Littles, Tiny Organics).
4. **One pill-shaped primary button, sticky at the bottom on phones** (Tiny Organics, Little Spoon).
5. **Horizontal rows of cards with "See all"** for Make again and Try this alternatives (Mealime).
6. **Short, warm microcopy** that reassures without advising (Tiny Organics, Feeding Littles).
7. **Small help icons next to facts** that explain a term (Solid Starts' "Iron-rich ⓘ").

## Patterns to avoid

Long disclaimer blocks above content, ad and app-download banners, chat bubbles, sale banners, carousels, stock photos, and checkbox walls.

## Not reviewed

Supercook and BLW Meals blocked automated browsing, and Yummy Toddler Food returned a bot check.
