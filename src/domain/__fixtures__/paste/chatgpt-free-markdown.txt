# Sweet Potato and Lentil Mash

Here is a soft, iron-rich mash for a baby who is ready for thicker purees.

**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 20 minutes
**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and diced
- ½ cup red lentils
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1-2 tbsp olive oil
- Pinch of cinnamon
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley (optional)

## Instructions

1. Boil the sweet potato and lentils in the **water** for 20 minutes.
2. Drain, add the oil and mash until the texture is smooth.
3. Stir in the cinnamon.

### Tips

- Freeze in small portions.
- Reheat until steaming hot, then let it cool.

Enjoy!
