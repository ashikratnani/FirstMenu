🍌 Banana oat bites for baby-led weaning! 🍌

✨ Ingredients: 1 ripe banana, 1/2 cup oats, 1 tbsp peanut butter, pinch cinnamon

👩‍🍳 Method:
1️⃣ Mash the banana
2️⃣ Stir in the oats and peanut butter
3️⃣ Bake for 12 min at 350°F
