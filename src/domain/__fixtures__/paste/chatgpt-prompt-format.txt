Title: Banana oat pancakes
Total time: 15 minutes
Serves: 2
Ingredients:
- 1 ripe banana
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp olive oil (optional)
Steps:
1. Mash the banana in a bowl until smooth.
2. Stir in the oats and the egg.
3. Heat the oil in a pan over low heat.
4. Cook small spoonfuls for 2 minutes per side, then let cool before serving.
