# FirstMenu

Meal Planner for Babies

A free web app for planning a week of baby meals for babies roughly 6 to 12 months old. Pick recipes by age stage or add your own, and get a weekly plan, a make-ahead prep list and a grocery list. Every recipe is screened against a fixed infant-safety list based on pediatric guidance.

**Status:** in planning. The spec is in [`.kiro/specs/first-menu/`](.kiro/specs/first-menu/).

FirstMenu is not medical advice. Check with your pediatrician.
